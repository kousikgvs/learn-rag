Microsoft is a multinational technology company whose core businesses span enterprise software, cloud computing, developer platforms, AI services, security, business applications, gaming, and devices. Its major operating segments remain Productivity and Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud, and More Personal Computing. Satya Nadella led the company through fiscal year 2024.

In fiscal year 2024, Microsoft reported $245.1 billion in revenue and $88.1 billion in net income, both records for the company. Management cited broad-based growth across all segments, with particularly strong performance in Azure, Microsoft Cloud, Office Commercial, and gaming. Microsoft Cloud revenue increased to $137.4 billion.

Fiscal year 2024 also reflected Microsoft's deeper transition from talking about AI to commercializing AI products at scale. The company expanded Copilot offerings, Azure AI services, data platform capabilities, and cloud infrastructure investments. The year also included the completed Activision Blizzard acquisition, which expanded Microsoft's gaming business and content portfolio.

Additional operating context for Microsoft in 2024:

Microsoft entered fiscal 2024 as one of the clearest examples of a large incumbent software company successfully repositioning around cloud and AI. The company still benefited from long-standing franchises such as Windows and Office, but more of the strategic narrative centered on Azure, Microsoft Cloud, Copilot, data platforms, cybersecurity, and developer tools. Intelligent Cloud covered Azure, server products, enterprise services, databases, analytics, and infrastructure software. In fiscal 2024, Microsoft pushed harder to monetize AI at scale. Copilot branding expanded across consumer and enterprise offerings, including productivity use cases, developer tooling, and cloud services. Azure remained the core infrastructure layer for model training, deployment, enterprise data integration, and AI application development. Microsoft also continued investing in custom silicon and data center capacity to support AI workloads.

Developer and enterprise ecosystems remained a major competitive advantage. GitHub, GitHub Copilot, Visual Studio, Azure DevOps, SQL Server, Power Platform, Dynamics 365, and security offerings reinforced Microsoft's presence across application development and enterprise operations.
